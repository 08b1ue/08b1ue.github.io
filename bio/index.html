Concept

A personal brand between precision and dissolution. One blue, #002AF7, with black, white, and generous negative space — grain, halftone, and particles as its visual language. Like the sea at night: quiet, yet always in motion.

Mission

To turn solitude, memory, and emotion into quiet visual spaces, where people can pause, look inward, and accept who they are.

Azure Cui

Motion Media Design student at SCAD.

I am an observer by nature — the sea, windows glowing at night, strangers passing by. Reading, music, and sociology keep me curious about people, identity, and relationships, and these quiet observations become the grain, particles, and flowing light in my work.

To me, design is a way of listening inward.
