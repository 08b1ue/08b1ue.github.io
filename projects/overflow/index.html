OVERFLOW

Mixed-Media Motion

MOME 105 | FALL 2026

A mixed-media motion piece about panic attacks. An ordinary moment spirals out of control and overflows to the rhythm of a heartbeat, showing what panic feels like from the inside and inviting viewers to meet it with empathy rather than judgement.

Research References:

Baker et al. (2022). Adolescents’ lived experience of panic disorder: an interpretative phenomenological analysis.

• Bodily sensations and fearful thoughts reinforce one another, creating an overwhelming cycle.

• Feelings of unreality, entrapment, and fear of being watched can intensify distress.

Hewitt et al. (2021). The experience of panic attacks in adolescents: an interpretative phenomenological analysis study.

• Participants described losing control and feeling that the attack would never end.

• Feelings of isolation and mental imagery contributed to the experience of panic.
